In this problem, a given dump trucks carrying capacity at the time of its production was 13,000 pounds. At a given point in the future, when loaded to a routine load of 12,000 pounds at a dump site, the dump trucks thrust rod used to lift and unload the bed of the truck failed. The above case is an example of which type of steel failure, fatigue, expansion, deformation, or deflection. This is an example of a conceptual problem that we would not be able to use the reference manual to help us with. Instead, it is a case where we would need to understand what is happening in the case provided and recognize the type of failure that occurred. In this case, the problem states that the dump truck is routinely loaded to 12,000 pounds, which is less than its maximum carrying capacity of 13,000 pounds. The fact that this is the same capacity at which the thrust rod failed can be understood to be an example of fatigue. Fatigue type failures are due to a reduction in strength due to cyclical loading. The problem does not provide any more detail as to what exactly happened to the thrust rod, so it would be a stretch to guess that any of the other type of failures listed here were the cause.
