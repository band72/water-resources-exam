In this problem, a given soil sample has been contaminated by an oil spill. The tuine present in the oil is biodegrading according to the first order kinetics at a constant rate of 0.15% per day. If the initial tuine concentration in the soil is 22 mg per kilogram, by how much will the touine concentration be decreased by in milligs per kilogram after 2 years? So the first words here to take note of in this problem are first order and we need to recognize that this is a decay problem. Searching decay in the reference manual will lead us to a list of equations in chapter 5. This will lead us to a first order decay equation which we can fill in every term for except for the one we are searching for. So first order decay is going to be given as CT equals CO E raised to the minus K * T. CT is going to be our final concentration which we don't have yet. CO is going to be our initial concentration which we can see is given as 22 mg per kilogram. K is our decay rate which is going to be equal to 0.15% per day. And note that this is going to be expressed as a decimal. So 0.0015 over days. And then finally t is our time component which we can see is going to be 2 years. Note that since K is in days, we were going to need to convert years to days here as well. When in doubt, remember to follow your units. So, this equation is quick to set up and quick to solve. Let's plug in our known and solve for our unknown. CT= 22 mg per kilogram time E raised to the minus 0. 015 over day time 700 30 day. And our units will cancel and leave us with a final concentration of 7.35 mg per kilogram. Finally, although we have an answer and one that's even listed here, this is not going to be the one we want to pick. The problem is asking us by how much will the towing concentration be decreased by after 2 years. We are looking for the amount the concentration decreased by between time 0 and time t. That decrease is going to be equal to the initial concentration subtracted by the concentration at time t. So this will be 22 mg per kilogram minus 7.35 mg per kilogram or 14.65 mg per kg which is not our second answer but rather our third. So in summary I think this is a rather easy problem.
