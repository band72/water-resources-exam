The compressive strength of the previous slab was 3,400 psi, and the designers want to replace the slab with a mix of equal or greater strength. What are the most likely materials to be included in the concrete mix? Type 2 cement, chemical accelerator, coarse aggregate, and sand, type 3 cement, chemical accelerator, sand and water, type 3 cement, chemical accelerator, coarse aggregate, sand and water, or type 5 cement, chemical accelerator, coarse aggregate, sand and water.
