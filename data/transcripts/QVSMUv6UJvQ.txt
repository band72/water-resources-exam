Drum A produces a radiation intensity of 80 mR per hour at its surface and is located 4 ft center to monitor from the monitor. Drum B produces a radiation intensity of 180 mR per hour at its surface and is located on the opposite side of the monitor at an unknown distance center to monitor. If the total radiation measured at the monitor is 10 mR per hour, what is the distance in feet from the centers of drum A and drum B?
